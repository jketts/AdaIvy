Kelvin Normalisation Cadence: Primary Statement

Theorem A. Let the kelvin cadence operator act on a finite index set. If the
cadence weights are strictly positive, then the normalisation step preserves the
total cadence mass at every iteration.

Proof sketch. The cadence mass is a linear functional of the weights, and the
normalisation step rescales all weights by a single positive factor. Therefore
the functional is invariant.

Remark. The argument depends on the sundial support threshold established
elsewhere, and it admits a reformulation in the language of majorisation.
