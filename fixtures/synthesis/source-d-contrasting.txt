Quasi Stochastic Majorisation: A Contrasting Route

Proposition D. Every quasi stochastic rescaling on a finite index set induces a
majorisation ordering, and the ordering is preserved under composition.

Proof sketch. Majorisation is characterised by partial sums of the ordered
vector, and quasi stochastic rescaling acts monotonically on those partial sums.

Remark. This route reaches a comparable conclusion without any cadence
hypothesis, and therefore contrasts with the primary statement.
