# ADR-NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | rejected | superseded
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Blueprint requirement:** section/invariant/phase
- **Decision owners:** names or roles

## Context

Describe the bounded problem, constraints, and evidence. Link reproducible spike
results and blockers. Distinguish verified facts from assumptions.

## Options considered

| Option | Evidence | Benefits | Costs/risks | Hard gates |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Decision

State the chosen option and exact boundary. Identify what remains canonical and
what may be delegated.

## Consequences

List operational, security, licensing, reproducibility, migration, and testing
effects, including negative consequences.

## Blueprint deviation

Write `None` or identify the exact deviation, necessity, and revisit trigger.
Never hide a deviation in implementation detail.

## Validation and revisit trigger

Define the checks that keep the decision valid and the evidence that would
cause reconsideration.
